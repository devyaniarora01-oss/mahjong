Mahjong Coach

  Mahjong Coach is a beginner-friendly Mahjong game that teaches you by letting you play.

  Instead of expecting players to know the rules in advance, the game explains the table, tiles,
  rounds, hand patterns, claims, scoring, and decisions as they happen.

  ## Play online

  [Open Mahjong Coach](https://mahjong.vercel.app)

  ## What it includes

  - Four-player Mahjong table with three automated opponents
  - Beginner onboarding that explains setup and gameplay
  - East, South, West, and North rounds
  - Visual tile rendering for Bamboo, Characters, Dots, Winds, Dragons, Flowers, and Seasons
  - Draw Wall, Flower Wall, bonus tiles, discards, exposed melds, and claims
  - Charleston exchange and South Card objectives
  - East, South, West, and North round-specific gameplay
  - Score tracking across all four rounds
  - Explain My Hand coaching
  - Best Move suggestions based on your visible hand and round objective
  - End-of-round score summaries and game review

  ## Game flow

  A full game has four directional rounds:
  1. East Round
  2. South Round
  3. West Round
  4. North Round

  Scores carry forward between rounds. At the end of North Round, the game shows the final standings.

  ## Teaching-first design

  Mahjong Coach is designed for players who may never have seen Mahjong before.

  The game teaches:

  - What the 144 tiles are
  - How the table is set up
  - Why East begins with 14 tiles
  - What the Draw Wall and Flower Wall do
  - How draws, discards, claims, Flowers, Seasons, Pungs, Pure Chows, Kongs, Crochets, Knits, and
    Pairs work

  - What your hand is building toward
  - Why a suggested discard may be useful
