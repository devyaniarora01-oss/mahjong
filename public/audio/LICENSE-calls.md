# Call-out voices

`calls/*.mp3` are converted from the voice packs of KDE Kajongg
(https://invent.kde.org/games/kajongg), recordings by Wolfgang Rohdewald,
licensed GPL v2 or later. Voices: east=female1, south=male1, west=female2, north=male2.
If this app is distributed beyond a demo, comply with the GPL or replace these clips.
